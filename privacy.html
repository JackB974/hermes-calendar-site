Privacy policy

Hermes Calendar is a personal-use calendar assistant operated by JackB974. It accesses Google Calendar information to respond to the owner's requests to view and manage calendar events.

Information and use

Event details, such as titles, dates, times and reminders, are read or modified when needed for the requested calendar operation. Requests and responses are communicated through Discord and processed by the configured assistant and service providers. Calendar data may appear in conversation history and operational logs on the assistant's VPS or in connected services, depending on their settings.

Access and storage

Google OAuth credentials are stored on the operator's VPS to allow the integration to access the authorized calendar. Google Calendar continues to hold the calendar events. This public website is an informational site, not a calendar data entry form; do not post private information or credentials in public GitHub issues.

Control and deletion

The account owner can review or revoke the app's Google Account access through Google Account settings. Revoking access stops future access but does not itself delete existing calendar events, conversation history or local records; those must be removed from their respective services or VPS.

Contact

For a general question, open an issue on the project contact page. Issues are public; do not include sensitive information.
