Hermes Calendar

Hermes Calendar is a personal calendar assistant operated by JackB974. It helps its owner view and manage Google Calendar events through a private Discord conversation.

When the owner requests it and confirms changes, the assistant uses Google Calendar access to read, create, update or delete events. It requests Calendar access for these functions.

This is a personal-use integration, not a public calendar service. See the privacy policy and terms.

Questions: use the contact page. Issues are public; do not post calendar details or credentials.
